About the Stay

Your peaceful corner in the Kumaon Himalayas

Perched quietly above the valley in Dinapani, Woodpecker's Nest is thoughtfully designed for people who want to slow down without giving up comfort. Whether you're a couple seeking a retreat, a remote worker craving clarity, or simply someone who needs to reset — this place was made for you.

Mornings here begin with golden sunrises from your balcony, painting the Himalayan ridgeline in amber and rose before the world wakes up. Evenings wind down with cool mountain air, complete silence, and a sky so full of stars you lose count.

Hosted by Deepak — a traveller and engineer from Bangalore — this isn't just a rental. It's a space created with genuine intent, where every detail is designed to help you feel at ease.
